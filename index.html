1. Pick dice

Select a Pair, Triplet, Run, or another hand below.

2. Check the preview

Chips × Mult tells you the points your selection would earn. Selecting spends nothing.

3. Play hand

Bank those points and use one scoring hand. Or hold useful dice and reroll the rest.

Example: two 4s → (8 base Chips + 4 + 4) × 2 Mult = 32 points

Blue = Chips. Red = Mult (multiplier). Gold = points. Studies improve a hand’s base; Dice Gods add bonuses or multiply it.

Hands, colors and scoring
  • Choose a hand. Select Pair (two matching values), Two Pair (two different pairs), Triplet, Quads, a Run of three or four sequential values, or Color Pact (three or four dice of one effective color). Submit at most four dice, even if your tray holds up to eight. Runs cannot wrap. If the whole tray has no regular hand, Highest Die is a weak upgradable fallback. The preview chooses the highest-scoring classification of your selected dice.
  • Build a hand. Hand base Chips/Mult, Studies, matching-color bonuses, combos, action boosts and vouchers prepare the score. Pair receives +1 Mult for a matching color; other matching-color hands receive +2. Pure Run adds +6 Chips and a final ×1.25; Prism Run adds +4 Chips/+1 Mult; Twin Pacts adds +8 Chips/+1 Mult. Studies and current levels are under Hands.
  • Dice fire, then Gods. Selected dice fire in selection order. Each adds pips, permanent Chip bonuses and die enhancements. Die-scoring Gods fire left to right within each trigger. Crowned faces are the top two natural faces: 5–6 on d6, 3–4 on d4. Portrait gives ×2 each time the first Crowned die scores. Beacon gives +2 Mult whenever your first die scores. After initial dice, after-hand Gods fire left to right. Striker gives the first die two extra triggers, replaying its eligible scoring effects. A Crowned first die with Portrait and Striker produces ×2 ×2 ×2 = ×8 Mult. Retriggers never recursively fire other retrigger Gods. Refrain repeats the preceding active after-hand God’s immediate effects once; it skips paint and passive die-scoring Gods and never duplicates growth. Put additive effects before multipliers. Select dice to see their details and the Crowned definition. All die details shows paint, enhancements and weights. Tap Gods to inspect or move them; selection order matters too. Best hand checks useful lead-die orders.
Holding, actions and paint
  • Hold and reroll. Holding preserves both value and color. Unheld dice reroll both; permanent Seals lock only color. Scoring or burning clears holds and rolls the full tray. All rerolls, including free ones, count for reroll-sensitive Gods. Small Bones uses 1–4 faces; Loaded and face-setting actions respect the die’s maximum.
  • Actions and paint. Select one die for single-die actions; Carbon Copy, Borrowed Hue and Trading Places need two dice. Seals permanently lock paint, Break the Seal unlocks it, Washes temporarily override it, Clean Slate removes a Wash, and Wild Pigment rolls an unlocked color. Priority: Wash → color God → palette license → underlying rolled or locked color. Haunted Bones starts with Haunt and unlocks Ritual offers: Haunt gives ×1.5 die Chips, Radiance costs an additional $4 for permanent +2 Mult per die trigger, Severance sacrifices your rightmost God to enhance the remaining dice. Rituals replay through retriggers where applicable.
Shops, Studies and evolution
  • Shop and specialize. Normal markets have two Gods, one random Study, one action and one voucher. Recent offers have cooldowns. God offers first roll rarity: 65% common, 25% uncommon, 9% rare, 1% legendary, redistributed if a tier is unavailable. Powerful common combinations can carry a run. Grand Bazaar and Foresight add choices. Extra dice retain legendary rarity, Ante 2 gating and the eight-die cap. Vouchers shows only owned upgrades and purchase levels. Echo Chamber tokens can optionally duplicate a purchased Study or action; free an action slot first.
  • Grow your engine. Echo grows with Pairs; Wayfarer grows +0.5 Mult per Run; Pactkeeper grows +0.5 Mult per Color Pact, then multiplies. Growth belongs to that God and resets on sale. Evolving Gods show goals on their cards. Four Pillars needs three Quads; other current goals need five relevant scoring hands. Previews and copied effects do not advance goals. Choose one evolution path per God per run.
Rounds, bosses and saving
  • Finish the round. Beat the target before scoring hands run out. Unused score does not carry. Clears pay a base reward, $2 per unused hand and interest ($1 per $5 held, normally capped at $5). Working Fortune replaces this with $3 per unused hand and $1 per unused reroll, without interest. Unused rerolls reset. Casual adds 100 to targets per round; Hard has a smooth rising curve, with bosses at the end of Antes 3 and 6. No extra post-boss doubling. Equilibrium balances final Chips and Mult to their average and doubles all targets. Inspect the Stakes map for exact targets.

Full animation explains each scoring step; Instant skips the animation. Both play the same hand. Your run saves automatically on this device.
