THE HOUSE RULES

Dice. Gods.
Questionable decisions.

Beat 24 rounds across eight Antes. Each round has a target, three scoring hands, and three shared rerolls. Every scoring hand gives the whole tray a fresh roll for free. Leftover score does not carry to the next round.

  1. Choose a hand. Select matching values for Pair (2), Triplet (3), Quads (4), with a fixed limit of four dice per scoring hand. More tray dice give more choices, never a larger submitted hand. Quints and higher ranks are not submittable with this limit. Two Pair needs exactly four dice with two different pairs. Runs need three or four distinct sequential values. A Color Pact needs three or four dice of the same effective color; values may differ. The four original die colors are red, black, blue, and gold. If a selection qualifies for several hands, the preview automatically picks its highest score with your current upgrades and Gods. A run cannot wrap from 6 to 1. If the whole tray contains no regular hand, Highest Die automatically scores the highest face value ×1. It has no matching-color bonus. Its Study adds only +2 Chips and +0.25 Mult per level. Full houses are not numerical hands, though same-color selections can form Color Pacts. Select a valid subset of a bigger roll; only four dice may be submitted.
  2. Chips × Mult = score. Your selected dice add their pip values and permanent chip bonuses to the hand’s base Chips. All dice the same effective color add +2 Mult, except against The Pale House. Hand upgrades and Dice Gods add more.
  3. Hold and reroll. Select dice and tap “Hold selected” to keep their values. Reroll changes every unheld die and spends one reroll. Holds don’t automatically select scoring dice. Scoring or burning a hand clears all holds and rolls the whole tray. Score FX optionally counts the base hand, each die, color bonus, and Gods in order. The animation can be skipped; turning it off scores immediately. “Find best hand” selects the highest scoring available group.
  4. Visit the market. Reaching the target immediately clears the round. Earn a base payout, $2 per unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 held (up to $5). The market normally offers two Dice Gods, one Study, one action card, and one voucher. Studies are random and recent offers have a cooldown, so Pair Study is not guaranteed. Foresight adds one optional extra God offer. Buy Dice Gods, strengthen available hands, collect actions, or buy permanent run vouchers. Refreshing the shop starts at $2 and rises each time.
  5. Build your engine. Three dice, four Gods, and three action cards fit at first. Gods can be sold in the shop for half their price, rounded down (65% with Better Bargain). The Echo loses its accumulated Mult when sold; rebuying starts it from zero. Extra-die offers are legendary, cost more, and do not appear until Ante 2. Extra-die vouchers add up to four dice; The Sixth Finger God adds one more, for a maximum tray of eight. Extra-hand and reroll vouchers can each be bought twice; one slot voucher gives a fifth God slot. Hidden Sleeve unlocks a fourth action slot. Other vouchers discount purchases, raise rewards or interest, improve God selection, or polish dice each Ante.
  6. Actions matter. For single-die cards, select exactly one die before tapping Use. Carbon Copy needs two dice selected in order: source first, destination second. Extra Breath, Last Chance, Overcharge, Fortune’s Favor, and Second Wind need no target. Blood & Ink cycles through the four original colors; Gold Leaf adds +6 scoring Chips; Loaded adds +1 to future rolls (capped at six, up to +2 per die). These changes last the run. Other actions change current values, add a hand/reroll, or boost the next scoring hand. Six Appeal only changes the current roll. Second Wind rerolls unheld dice for free. Discarding a card frees its slot.

Modes: Casual starts at 100 points and adds 100 every round, with no bosses. Hard has fast-growing targets, bosses, and a doubled target scale after each boss (×2 from Ante 4, ×4 from Ante 7). Choose a mode from the mode badge or New run; changing modes starts a fresh run.

Hard-mode bosses: The final round of Antes 3 and 6 is a boss. The Pale House (Ante 3) disables only the matching-color +2 Mult bonus; color-sensitive Gods still work. The Iron Hand (Ante 6) locks normal rerolls; Second Wind still works. A boss handicap ends when that round is cleared. Open the Stakes map for every target and upcoming handicap.

Two Pair: Select four dice with two different matching values, such as 2, 2, 5, 5. Base scoring is 24 Chips × 3 Mult, plus pips and bonuses. It has its own hand level. The Twins doubles its Mult once; The Echo grows by +2 Mult for it.

“Permanent” means for this run. Every new run resets dice, money, Gods, and upgrades. If you cannot make a regular hand, reroll, use an action, or submit Highest Die. Burning a hand remains an option for zero points. Running out of hands below target ends the run. No purchases or real money are involved.

Progress saves automatically. Eight Antes is a win; restarting replaces your saved run.
