THE HOUSE RULES

Dice. Gods.
Questionable decisions.

Beat 24 rounds across eight Antes. Each round has a target, three scoring hands, and three shared rerolls. Every scoring hand gives the whole tray a fresh roll for free. Leftover score does not carry to the next round.

  1. Choose a hand. Select exactly two equal dice for a Pair, exactly three equal dice for a Triplet, or three to six distinct sequential values for a Run. A run cannot wrap from 6 to 1. No singles, four-of-a-kind, full houses, or other poker hands. You can select a valid subset of a bigger roll.
  2. Chips × Mult = score. Your selected dice add their pip values and permanent chip bonuses to the hand’s base Chips. All dice the same effective color add +2 Mult. Hand upgrades and Dice Gods add more.
  3. Hold and reroll. Select dice and tap “Hold selected” to keep their values. Reroll changes every unheld die and spends one reroll. Holds don’t automatically select scoring dice. Scoring or burning a hand clears all holds and rolls the whole tray. “Find best hand” selects the highest scoring available group.
  4. Visit the market. Reaching the target immediately clears the round. Earn a base payout, $2 per unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 held (up to $5). Buy Dice Gods, upgrade Pair/Triplet/Run levels, collect actions, or buy permanent run vouchers. Refreshing the shop starts at $2 and rises each time.
  5. Build your engine. Four Gods and three action cards fit at first. Gods can be sold in the shop for half their price, rounded down. Extra-die vouchers add up to three dice; The Sixth Finger God adds one more, for a maximum tray of eight. Extra-hand and reroll vouchers can each be bought twice; one slot voucher gives a fifth God slot.
  6. Actions matter. For single-die cards, select exactly one die before tapping Use. Blood & Ink switches its original color; Gold Leaf adds +6 scoring Chips; Loaded adds +1 to future rolls (capped at six, up to +2 per die). These changes last the run. Six Appeal only changes the current roll. Second Wind rerolls unheld dice for free. Discarding a card frees its slot.

“Permanent” means for this run. Every new run resets dice, money, Gods, and upgrades. If you can’t make a hand, reroll, use an action, or burn a hand. Running out of hands below target ends the run. No purchases or real money are involved.

Progress saves automatically. Eight Antes is a win; restarting replaces your saved run.
