THE HOUSE RULES

Dice. Gods.
Questionable decisions.

Beat 24 rounds across eight Antes. Each round has a target, three scoring hands, and three shared rerolls. Every scoring hand gives the whole tray a fresh roll for free. Leftover score does not carry to the next round.

  1. Choose a hand. Select matching values for Pair (2), Triplet (3), Quads (4), with a fixed limit of four dice per scoring hand. More tray dice give more choices, never a larger submitted hand. Quints and higher ranks are not submittable with this limit. Two Pair needs exactly four dice with two different pairs. Runs need three or four distinct sequential values. A Color Pact needs three or four dice of the same effective color; values may differ. The four original die colors are red, black, blue, and gold. If a selection qualifies for several hands, the preview automatically picks its highest score with your current upgrades and Gods. A run cannot wrap from 6 to 1. If the whole tray contains no regular hand, Highest Die automatically scores the highest face value ×1. It has no matching-color bonus. Its Study adds only +2 Chips and +0.25 Mult per level. Full House needs five dice and does not fit the four-die cap. Pure Run (same-color Run) adds +6 Chips and ×1.25 Mult; Prism Run (four-color Run of four) adds +4 Chips and +1 Mult; Twin Pacts (Two Pair with each pair matching in color) adds +8 Chips and +1 Mult. These combos use their underlying Run or Two Pair Studies and Gods. Combo additions precede multiplier effects; Pure Run’s multiplier lands after God multipliers. Glass Ceiling also weakens the Pure Run multiplier. Select a valid subset of a bigger roll; only four dice may be submitted.
  2. Chips × Mult = score. Your selected dice add their pip values and permanent chip bonuses to the hand’s base Chips. A Pair with both dice the same effective color adds +1 Mult; other matching-color hands add +2 Mult, except against The Pale House. Hand upgrades and Dice Gods add more.
  3. Hold and reroll. Select dice and tap “Hold selected” to keep their values. Reroll changes every unheld die and spends one reroll. Holds don’t automatically select scoring dice. Scoring or burning a hand clears all holds and rolls the whole tray. Scoring happens on the table: dice count Chips, Gods light up, Mult grows, then points and winnings flow into your totals. Choose slower Full animation or Instant scoring. Full pauses on each contribution and multiplier hit. Sound is optional and saved separately. Tap Skip scoring to finish immediately. A confirmed hand is saved before its presentation, so reloading never scores it twice. “Find best hand” selects the highest scoring available group.
  4. Visit the market. Reaching the target immediately clears the round. Earn a base payout, $2 per unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 held (up to $5). The market normally offers two Dice Gods, one Study, one action card, and one voucher. Studies are random and recent offers have a cooldown, so Pair Study is not guaranteed. Foresight adds one optional extra God offer. Buy Dice Gods, strengthen available hands, collect actions, or buy permanent run vouchers. Refreshing the shop starts at $2 and rises each time.
  5. Build your engine. Four dice, four Gods, and three action cards fit at first. Gods can be sold in the shop for half their price, rounded down (65% with Better Bargain). The Echo loses its accumulated Mult when sold; rebuying starts it from zero. Extra-die offers are legendary, cost more, and do not appear until Ante 2. Extra-die vouchers and The Sixth Finger add dice up to the maximum tray of eight. Extra-hand and reroll vouchers can each be bought twice; one slot voucher gives a fifth God slot. Hidden Sleeve unlocks a fourth action slot. Other vouchers discount purchases, raise rewards or interest, improve God selection, or polish dice each Ante.
  6. Actions matter. For single-die cards, select exactly one die before tapping Use. Carbon Copy needs two dice selected in order: source first, destination second. Extra Breath, Last Chance, Overcharge, Fortune’s Favor, and Second Wind need no target. Blood & Ink cycles through the four original colors; Gold Leaf adds +6 scoring Chips; Loaded adds +1 to future rolls (capped at six, up to +2 per die). These changes last the run. Other actions change current values, add a hand/reroll, or boost the next scoring hand. Six Appeal only changes the current roll. Second Wind rerolls unheld dice for free. Discarding a card frees its slot.

God chains and order: After base scoring, color/combos, actions and vouchers, active Gods fire in rack order, left to right. Tap a God and use Move left/right, or drag it on desktop. +Mult before ×Mult is stronger than the reverse. Paint rules apply upfront regardless of slot; Pure Run’s multiplier follows the Gods. The Striker repeats the first scoring die’s pips and permanent Chip bonus once. The Resonator adds +2 Mult per die repeat that already happened. The Refrain repeats the preceding active God’s immediate effects once, skipping paint/inactive Gods. It cannot duplicate permanent growth or evolution progress, and repeats never recursively fire other Gods. The Oath offers ×2 Mult at the cost of one future-round reroll while equipped.

Evolution: Echo, Wayfarer, Ember and Kaleidoscope each evolve after five relevant hands while equipped. Their cards show progress; tap EVOLVE to choose one of two permanent paths for this run. Previews, copied triggers and rerolls do not advance goals. Selling clears that God’s evolution. Current saved builds keep their progress and rack order, with new evolution goals starting at zero.

Colors: Dice keep their original colors between rolls. Tap the palette beside the round title to see all four color counts, original die colors, and active overrides. Round Washes take priority over color-changing Gods, then palette licenses, then original colors. Washes last until the round ends. Four Seals permanently repaint a selected die red, black, blue, or gold. Red Queen, Ink King, Blue Regent, and Golden Sovereign each force one color; The Divide restricts dice to red/black. Only one color-changing God fits at a time. Blood & Ink or Ocean & Ore licenses restrict the natural palette for the run; choose only one. Spectrum Grant and The Kaleidoscope reward varied colors.

Modes: Casual starts at 100 points and adds 100 every round, with no bosses. Hard has fast-growing targets, bosses, and a doubled target scale after each boss (×2 from Ante 4, ×4 from Ante 7). Choose a mode from the mode badge or New run; changing modes starts a fresh run.

Hard-mode bosses: The final round of Antes 3 and 6 is a boss. A d20 roll selects one of 20 saved handicaps on entry. Colors, particular hands, God effects, rerolls, scoring hands, or the submission limit can be affected. The selected boss never rerolls on reload. Open Card collection → 20 Bosses to inspect every handicap. A boss handicap ends when that round is cleared. Open the Stakes map for every target and upcoming handicap.

Probability balance: Base rewards account for four fair dice and optimal holds/rerolls. On one roll: Pair 72.22%, Run of 3+ 27.78%, Triplet 9.72%, Two Pair 6.94%, Run of 4 5.56%, Quads 0.46%. These overlap: a Quad also contains a Triplet and Pair. With one reroll dedicated to chasing that hand: 92.28%, 57.58%, 31.52%, 19.74%, 18.95%, and 3.87% respectively. Your three rerolls are shared across the round. Runs start at 6 Chips ×3; a fourth die adds +4 base Chips. Triplet starts at 18 ×3, Two Pair at 20 ×3, Quads at 72 ×5. Color Pact starts at 8 ×1.5: fixed colors make it guaranteed once you build a matching-color tray. Short Runs and guaranteed color hands scale more slowly; Quads and Two Pair have bigger Study gains. Read the odds and balance method ↗

Pair balance: Pair starts at 8 Chips ×2 Mult. Pair Study adds +6 Chips and +0.5 Mult; its same-color bonus is +1 Mult. The Twins gives Pair ×1.5, and The Looking Glass gives same-color Pair ×1.25. Echo grows +0.5 per Pair and +1 per Two Pair. Studies: Pair +6 Chips/+0.5 Mult; Run +8/+0.5; Triplet +12/+1; Two Pair +14/+1; Quads +20/+1.5; Color Pact +6/+0.5; Highest Die +2/+0.25.

Two Pair: Select four dice with two different matching values, such as 2, 2, 5, 5. Base scoring is 20 Chips × 3 Mult, plus pips and bonuses. It has its own hand level. The Twins doubles its Mult once; The Echo grows by +1 Mult for it.

“Permanent” means for this run. Every new run resets dice, money, Gods, and upgrades. If you cannot make a regular hand, reroll, use an action, or submit Highest Die. Burning a hand remains an option for zero points. Running out of hands below target ends the run. No purchases or real money are involved.

Progress saves automatically. Eight Antes is a win; restarting replaces your saved run.
