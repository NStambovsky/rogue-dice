# Rogue Dice — rules and first-run tutorial

## Choose a run

Tap the mode badge or **New run**. Casual starts at 100 points and adds 100 per round, with no bosses. Hard has escalating targets, two bosses, and permanent jumps in the stakes scale after each boss. Both modes contain eight Antes of three rounds each. Starting replaces your current run; cancel to keep it.

## Make a hand

Select only the dice you want to score. You do not need to use the whole tray.

| Hand | Dice | Base Chips | Base Mult |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Pair | Two equal values | 12 | 2 |
| Two Pair | Four dice: two different matching values | 24 | 3 |
| Triplet | Three equal values | 24 | 3 |
| Run | Three to six distinct consecutive values | 18 | 3 |

Two Pair: 2, 2, 5, 5 works; 4, 4, 4, 4 does not. A run cannot wrap from 6 to 1. Single dice, full houses, and four-of-a-kind cannot score as a whole, but valid subsets can. Each extra die beyond three in a Run adds +12 base Chips and +1 Mult.

**Chips × Mult = points.** Selected dice add their pip values and permanent chip upgrades. Matching effective colors add +2 Mult, except during The Pale House boss. Each hand upgrade adds +12 base Chips and +1 Mult to its own hand type.

A mixed-color Two Pair of 2, 2, 5, 5 scores (24 + 14) × 3 = **114**. All one color scores 38 × 5 = **190**. The Twins doubles Two Pair’s Mult once; The Echo grows by 2 for the two pairs.

## Keep useful dice

Tap dice to select them, then **Hold selected** to preserve their values during a reroll. Holds and scoring selection are separate. **Reroll unheld** spends one of the round’s three rerolls. **Find best hand** selects the highest-scoring legal subset, accounting for upgrades, Gods, and the current boss.

Score a hand to spend one of your three scoring hands. If the target is not yet reached, the entire tray rolls fresh for free and all holds clear. Rerolls do not replenish until the next round. If stuck, use an action, reroll, or **Burn a hand** for fresh dice without scoring. Running out of hands below target ends the run.

## Watch the stakes

The strip above the table shows the prior target → current target and upcoming stakes. Tap **Stakes map** for all targets in your selected mode. The shop previews the next target before you enter it.

Hard bosses occur on the final rounds of every third Ante:

- **Ante 3: The Pale House.** The matching-color +2 Mult bonus is disabled. Color-sensitive Gods still work.
- **Ante 6: The Iron Hand.** Normal rerolls are locked. Second Wind still works; scoring/burning hands still gives fresh dice.

These handicaps last only for that boss round. After each boss, Hard’s target scale doubles: ×2 from Ante 4, ×4 from Ante 7. Casual has neither bosses nor these jumps.

## Build between rounds

Clearing a round immediately opens the market. Earn a $6/$7/$8 base reward, $2 per unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 held, capped at $5. Excess score does not carry forward.

Buy Gods for ongoing effects, hand upgrades, action cards, and vouchers for additional dice, hands, rerolls, or one extra God slot. Shop refreshes start at $2 and increase each time. Gods can be sold in the shop for half price rounded down. The Sixth Finger uses a God slot and adds a die; selling it removes that die and its modifications. Vouchers can expand the tray to seven dice without Gods, or eight with The Sixth Finger.

Actions are consumed when used. Select exactly one die for Six Appeal (temporary 6), Blood & Ink (permanent original-color switch), Gold Leaf (+6 permanent scoring Chips), or Loaded (+1 to future rolls capped at 6, maximum +2 load). Second Wind rerolls unheld dice for free and needs no selected die. Permanent means for this run. A new run resets everything.

## Your first round

1. Start Casual. Your target is 100 with three hands and three rerolls.
2. Tap **Find best hand**, then inspect the score. A Pair of mixed-color fours scores 40; a matching-color Pair scores 80.
3. Hold useful dice and reroll the others if you want to improve the group. Another four could turn that Pair into a 108-point mixed-color Triplet.
4. Score your chosen hand. If below target, continue with the fresh tray. Points from several hands add toward the target.
5. In the market, choose an upgrade that supports the groups you are making. Check the next target before continuing.
6. In landscape on a small phone, dice and controls fit together. Tap a God or action name for its description; **Score breakdown** explains every scoring modifier.

Progress saves automatically on this device. Earlier saves retain their build and are labeled Hard; choose a fresh Casual run if you want linear targets.
