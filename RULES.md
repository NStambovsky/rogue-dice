# Rogue Dice — rules & tutorial

Start with **three six-sided dice** drawn from four original colors: **red, black, blue, and gold**. Your tray may eventually grow to eight dice, but you may submit **at most four dice per scoring hand**. Extra dice improve your choices, not the submission limit.

## Scoring hands

| Hand | Requirement | Base Chips | Base Mult |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Highest Die | Fallback only when the tray has no other legal hand | 0 | 1 |
| Pair | Two equal values | 12 | 2 |
| Two Pair | Four dice: two different pairs | 24 | 3 |
| Triplet | Three equal values | 24 | 3 |
| Quads | Four equal values | 48 | 5 |
| Run | Three or four distinct consecutive values | 18 | 3 |
| Color Pact | Three or four dice of one effective color, any values | 16 | 2 |

A four-die Run adds +12 base Chips and +1 Mult. Runs cannot wrap from 6 to 1. Quints and higher groups cannot be submitted under the four-die limit. A larger tray can still provide a four-die Quad subset.

Selected dice add their pip values and permanent chip bonuses. All-one-color hands of at least two dice add +2 Mult. Gods and one-shot boosts then apply; all additive bonuses are counted before Mult multipliers. The final score is floored once.

When a selection qualifies as both a numerical hand and Color Pact, the preview uses whichever scores higher with your current levels, Gods, and boss. Find Best Hand checks all legal subsets up to four dice.

**Highest Die** automatically uses the tray’s highest face only if there is no regular hand anywhere in the tray. Its initial score is just that face value ×1, with no single-die color bonus. Ordinary Studies add +12 Chips and +1 Mult; Highest Die Study adds only +2 Chips and +0.25 Mult. It takes many levels to become strong.

## Play a round

Each round starts with three scoring hands and three shared rerolls unless upgraded. Select dice, then **Hold selected** to preserve them on rerolls. Scoring selection and holds are separate. You can hold more than four dice by selecting and holding separate groups.

Reroll changes unheld dice and spends a reroll. Scoring spends a hand and gives the whole tray a fresh roll for free if the target remains unmet. Holds clear; rerolls do not replenish until the next round. Burn a hand for a fresh tray without points if desired. Reaching the target immediately clears the round; running out of hands below target ends the run.

## Score FX

Toggle **Score FX** beside the hold/best-hand controls. When on, a skippable walkthrough counts the base hand, each scoring die, color bonus, actions, God additions, multipliers, and final points. The exact same calculation supplies the preview and committed score. The preference saves on this device; off scores immediately.

## Build from what the market offers

A normal shop has **five offers**: two Gods, one Study, one action, and one voucher. Foresight adds one extra God offer. Recent offerings have cooldowns; the Study cannot be one of the last two offered Studies while alternatives remain. Pair Study is not guaranteed. Only hands that fit your tray and four-die cap receive Study offers.

Clearing pays a $6/$7/$8 base reward, $2 per unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 held up to $5. Vouchers can improve payouts, interest limits, prices, action capacity, God selection, resale, or permanent chip polishing. Refreshing costs $2 initially and rises by $1 each time, resetting next round.

The collection has **32 Dice Gods, 12 vouchers, and 15 action cards**. Open Rules → Card collection to inspect them. Gods use slots and work while equipped. Selling The Echo resets all its accumulated Mult; rebuying starts at zero. Selling The Sixth Finger removes its granted die, including modifications on that die.

Extra-die God/voucher offers are legendary and unavailable until Ante 2. The Sixth Finger lists at $16; A Bigger Fist starts at $18 and costs $8 more each repeat. Discounts may lower these prices. New runs can reach eight dice through four rare die vouchers and The Sixth Finger, but extra dice do not raise the hand limit.

## Actions

Each action is consumed on successful use. Most target one selected die. Carbon Copy needs two selected in order: source, then destination. Second Wind, Extra Breath, Last Chance, Overcharge, and Fortune’s Favor need no target.

Blood & Ink cycles original colors red → black → blue → gold. Red Seal and Black Seal set an original color. Gold Leaf adds permanent scoring Chips; Loaded biases rolls upward, capped at six and at +2 load per die. Overcharge and Fortune’s Favor affect the next scored hand and survive burns. Extra Breath and Last Chance add temporary resources for the current round. “Permanent” means for this run.

Color-changing Gods override effective colors while equipped without deleting the original colors. They can enable Color Pacts.

## Run modes

**Casual** starts at 100 and adds 100 to every next target, with no bosses. **Hard** has escalating targets. The third rounds of Antes 3 and 6 add a boss handicap; the target scale doubles afterward (×2 from Ante 4, ×4 from Ante 7).

The Pale House blocks the base matching-color +2 Mult bonus. The Iron Hand locks normal rerolls; Second Wind still works. Handicaps end after the boss round. Open Stakes Map for all targets and check the shop’s next-round preview before advancing. Beat all 24 rounds across eight Antes to win.

## First-run tutorial

1. Start Casual and look at the 100-point target.
2. Tap Find Best Hand. If you roll red 4, black 4, and blue 2, the Pair scores (12 + 4 + 4) ×2 = 40.
3. Hold the fours and reroll the other die to chase a Triplet. Or score the Pair and use the next free tray to keep building toward the target.
4. If no regular group exists, Score Highest Die gives a small fallback instead of forcing a zero-point burn.
5. Buy from the available market. Build around the Gods and Studies you actually see rather than assuming Pair Study will return.
6. A Color Pact of blue 1, 3, and 6 scores (16 + 10) ×4 = 104 before upgrades. Quads of mixed-color fours score (48 + 16) ×5 = 320.
7. Turn Score FX on to inspect each contribution. In landscape, tap God/action names and Score breakdown for details without leaving the compact table.

Existing saves retain their tray, money, mode, and owned upgrades. New hand levels and voucher fields are added automatically. Existing original dice colors are preserved; start a new run to get the three-die start drawn from the full four-color palette. Gods that force a color still display that effective color.
